Privacy Policy — Spike Guess

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Spike Guess is a Valorant quiz for iPhone and iPad. This policy explains what data the app processes, why, for how long, and what your rights are. In short: no ads, no tracking, no analytics, and no email or name requested.

Who is responsible

Sacha Vedy, independent developer and publisher of the app.

Contact: spikeguess8@gmail.com

Spike Guess is not affiliated with Riot Games.

Data we process

Your account

  • A technical account identifier, created automatically on first launch (anonymous account, no sign-up).
  • If you save your account with Sign in with Apple: only the identifier Apple provides for Spike Guess. The app does not ask Apple for your email address or your name.

Your nickname

  • The nickname you choose, followed by a number (e.g. "Kai#4821"). It is visible to your opponents in 1v1 and in their history.

Your games

  • Solo, 1v1 and daily challenge games: game settings, rounds played, answers typed or chosen, response times, points, lives, results, personal bests and daily challenge streak.

Technical data

  • To run the service and protect it against abuse, our hosting provider processes connection data (IP address, request times), and the app records rate-limit counters tied to your account.

On your device only

  • Your session (in the iOS keychain), your settings (language, vibrations, game preferences) and an image cache. This data is not sent to us and is removed with the app (the session may remain in the keychain).

What we do not collect

  • No location, contacts, photos, advertising identifier, analytics or diagnostics data. The app includes no advertising or audience measurement tools.

Why

  • To provide the game: create your account, draw questions, check your answers, compute points, run 1v1 games, show your history and personal bests (performance of the service you request).
  • To recover your account on another device or after reinstalling, if you chose Sign in with Apple (performance of the service).
  • Security, anti-cheat and abuse prevention: rate limits and technical logs (legitimate interest in protecting the service and other players).

Your data is never sold, rented or used for advertising.

Who we share it with

  • Supabase (database and server functions hosting), as a processor. The project is hosted in the European Union.
  • Apple, if you use Sign in with Apple: to authenticate you and, when you delete your account, to revoke the authorization granted to Spike Guess.
  • valorant-api.com: the app downloads game images (skins, agents) directly from it. Like any website, this service then receives your IP address. No other data is sent to it.
  • Your 1v1 opponents see your nickname and the game result.

How long

  • Account, nickname and history: as long as your account exists.
  • Unsaved (anonymous) inactive accounts: deleted automatically after 7 days without activity if no nickname was chosen, after 90 days otherwise. An account saved with Apple is never deleted automatically.
  • Typed answers in a finished game: 30 days. The game result stays in your history.
  • Abandoned solo games and expired 1v1 lobbies: 7 days.
  • Daily challenge attempts: 2 days (the challenge result stays in your history).
  • Hosting provider technical logs: limited period set by Supabase.

Deleting your account

In the app: Settings › Delete my account. Deletion is immediate and permanent: account, nickname, history, personal bests and streak are erased. If your account is linked to Apple, the authorization granted to Spike Guess is also revoked.

1v1 games remain in your opponent's history, but they are no longer tied to your account: your opponent sees "Deleted player" instead of your nickname.

Your rights

Under the GDPR, you can request access to your data, its correction (you can also change your nickname in Settings), its deletion (directly in the app), its portability, or object to processing based on legitimate interest.

Write to spikeguess8@gmail.com with your full nickname (e.g. "Kai#4821"): since we know neither your email nor your name, this is how we find your account. We reply within one month.

You can also lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority, the CNIL (www.cnil.fr), or with your local authority.

Children

Spike Guess asks for no identity information and is not specifically directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published at the same address with its update date. For significant changes, the app will let you know.
